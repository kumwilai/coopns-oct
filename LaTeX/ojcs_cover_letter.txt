Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, "Backbone-Modular Neuro-Symbolic Post-Correction for OCT Denoising with Explicit Predicate Maps and Stable Fuzzy Allocation," for publication in the IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society.

This work is submitted as a computing-methodology paper on transparent and safety-constrained post-correction for learned image restoration systems. Rather than proposing another OCT denoising backbone, the manuscript studies how a clinically meaningful correction policy can be attached to strong pretrained denoisers in a reproducible, auditable, and backbone-modular manner. The technical contribution is the coupling of three components into one formal wrapper: (1) explicit ground-truth-free predicate definitions with both scalar scores and spatial failure maps, (2) a differentiable fuzzy negotiator that routes correction through stable symbolic allocation, and (3) safety-gated correction rules with theorem-backed guarantees tied to the stated clinical objectives.

We believe OJ-CS is an appropriate venue because the central contribution is not limited to one medical dataset or one denoising architecture. The manuscript addresses a broader computer-systems and AI-methodology question: how to build reusable post-correction policies that remain interpretable and stable when attached to different learned backbones. OCT serves as a demanding application domain in which these properties are clinically meaningful and quantitatively verifiable.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission.

We respectfully request double-anonymous review. The paper is a substantially repositioned manuscript following prior handling elsewhere, and we request author anonymity during review to reduce the risk of identity-based bias while the work is evaluated on its technical merits.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Anonymous Authors
