\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Why fidelity falls, and when that is the right trade}
\label{sec:psnr_tradeoff}
Peak signal to noise ratio falls when the wrapper is applied, by 0.17 to 0.98 dB depending on the
backbone. A denoiser trained on squared error sits near a local optimum of that
error, so a change that raises contrast or boundary definition moves away from it. A few tenths of a
decibel is smaller than the variation between two acquisitions of the same eye, while the quantities
that improve are the ones a grader uses. The size of the gain tracks the room a
backbone leaves on each measure rather than its overall fidelity. On boundary sharpness NAFNet starts
lowest at 0.851 of the reference and gains most, and DnCNN starts at 1.031, above the reference, and
is the only backbone that falls. On edge preservation KBNet starts highest at 0.835 and is the only
backbone left unchanged. A deployment that must not lose fidelity can lower the gain bound
$\alpha_{\max}$, which attenuates the multiplicative branch, though the edge branch has its own bound
and only bypassing the wrapper recovers the backbone output exactly. Tightening the third constraint
would not do this, since the rule keeps the candidate in full whenever the other two hold.
SwinIR is the instructive case, since windowed attention smooths hard inside a window, leaving the
most room on contrast yet paying the least fidelity of the four at 0.17 dB.

\subsection{Limitations}
The correction is bounded by construction, so a backbone that has destroyed a structure cannot be
made to recover it. Two safety measures move against it on the test set. The invented edge rate rises
from \EdgeInvBack{} to \EdgeInvCorr{} percent and the retention of the weakest decile of structures
falls from \WeakRetBack{} to \WeakRetCorr{} percent, on \EdgeInvUp{} and \WeakRetDown{} of \NImages{} images. Neither is
zero, and the validation criterion that selected the operating
point scores six clinical measures and fidelity and does not see either of them. We measured what
that blindness costs, on the validation split so that the test split is used once. The setting ranked second for NAFNet
raises weak structure retention from 40.7 to 43.9 percent where the selected one lowers it to 37.7,
and adds less to the invented edge rate. The criterion is
therefore blind in a way that matters, and a user who values faint detail above contrast should score
these two measures during selection. We did not reselect on this evidence, because the criterion was
fixed before the test split was scored and changing it afterwards would spend that split twice. The clinical
measures used here are established proxies, not outcomes, and no clinician read the images, so the
safety analysis of Section~\ref{sec:safety_check} is quantitative evidence rather than clinical
validation. Finally, the external datasets are small, sixteen and eighteen images, so the transfer
results are evidence of behaviour across scanners rather than a precise estimate of the gain on any
one device.
