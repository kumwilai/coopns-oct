\section{Notation and Problem Statement}
\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Notation}
\label{sec:notation}
Images are real valued arrays of height $H$ and width $W$ with values in $[0,1]$. We write $\mathbf{y}$
for the noisy B-scan that comes off the scanner and $\mathbf{x}$ for the clean reference obtained by
averaging many repeated scans of the same location. A pixel index is written $p$, so $y_p$ is one
pixel of $\mathbf{y}$.

The symbol $\odot$ is the Hadamard product, $(\mathbf{t} \odot \mathbf{t}')_p = t_p t'_p$.
Table~\ref{tab:notation} lists the symbols that recur across sections, and a symbol used in one place
only is defined where it is introduced.

\subsection{The optimization problem}
\label{sec:optimization}
The denoiser is given and is not changed. The task is to produce an image that beats
$\mathbf{b} = f_\theta(\mathbf{y})$ on named clinical measures, stays close to it everywhere, and can
explain every change it makes. The only free quantities are the wrapper parameters $\phi$, namely the
cooperation head, the gain network, the edge network, the strength predictor and the rule layer, while
the backbone parameters $\theta$ are held fixed throughout. Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a set of paired scans
and $\hat{\mathbf{x}}(\phi)$ the output of the wrapper. We minimize the expected value of a weighted
sum of six terms with fixed weights $w_c$, $w_p$, $w_o$, $w_s$ and $w_r$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\min_{\phi}\;\; \mathbb{E}_{(\mathbf{y},\mathbf{x}) \sim \mathcal{D}}
\Big[\, &\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{fid}} + w_c \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{clin}}
       + w_p \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pred}} \\
      &+ w_o \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{coop}} + w_s \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{edge}}
       + w_r \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{reg}} \,\Big],
\end{split}
\label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{fid}}$ keeps the output near the clean reference,
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{clin}}$ rewards improvement of the clinical measures over the backbone,
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pred}}$ asks four property scores to rise above what the backbone already
reaches, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{coop}}$ ties the amount of correction to the estimated demand,
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{edge}}$ supervises the gradient field, and $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{reg}}$
collects the bounds on how large and how rough the change may be together with four terms that
protect the background. Section~\ref{sec:training} gives every term with its weight.

Three constraints are checked on every image and stated formally in Section~\ref{sec:safety}. The
aggregate clinical quality should not fall by more than a fixed tolerance, no single property should
fall by more than a fixed tolerance while another rises, and the relative change from the backbone
output should stay below a fixed bound. How many hold decides how much of the change is kept, so the
rule is a majority test and not three hard guarantees. Every logical connective is a piecewise linear
surrogate, so the objective is differentiable in $\phi$ including the rule layer and is solved with
AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019decoupled}. The constraints are not relaxed into the objective.
They are evaluated exactly, per image, at test time only, so the objective is optimized at
$\mathbf{q}$ and not at $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$, the acceptance rule not running while the wrapper is
fitted. The two coincide whenever the rule accepts in full, which
Section~\ref{sec:safety_check} reports is every test image.
