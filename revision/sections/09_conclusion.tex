\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a post-correction layer for OCT denoising, attached to a frozen denoiser rather than
replacing it. It states six clinical properties in closed form and turns them into an inspectable per-pixel
allocation for the multiplicative gain through five named rules, alongside an edge correction and a
background operation that do not pass through that allocation, and bounds how far any may move the
image. Across four backbone families the same rules, budgets and weights raise the clinical measures
at a fidelity cost below one decibel, and on three of the four no measure falls. The measures are proxies, and a reader study is the next step.
