\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Denoising of OCT images}
Learned denoisers for OCT fall into three families. Convolutional networks such as
DnCNN~\cite{zhang2017beyond} learn the noise as a residual and subtract it. Designs that gate or
reweight channels rather than pass them through a nonlinearity, such as NAFNet~\cite{chen2022simple}
and KBNet~\cite{zhang2023kbnet}, control what each contributes. Transformers such as
SwinIR~\cite{liang2021swinir} use windowed self attention. Methods built for OCT address the scarcity of paired data directly, through triplet cross
fusion~\cite{geng2022triplet} and sparse representation~\cite{fang2013fast}, although the earlier of
those still needs one high quality scan of the same eye~\cite{fang2012sparsity}.

All of these are trained to reduce a pixel error, so the quantity they optimize is not the quantity a
clinician reads. Work on quality assessment for OCT~\cite{wang2019quality} measures clinical quality
but does not act on it. This paper closes that loop, turning measured quality into a correction
applied under stated limits.

\subsection{Confidence and uncertainty in medical imaging}
Deep ensembles and their relatives~\cite{lakshminarayanan2017simple} are the standard way to attach
a confidence to a prediction. In OCT the networks that segment layers~\cite{devalla2018drunet} or
judge scan quality~\cite{wang2019quality} report an accuracy rather than an uncertainty, so a reader
learns how often the model is right and never where it is unsure, and the value is reported and then
left alone. We feed such a map into the correction decision instead, and
Section~\ref{sec:confidence_check} reports that the map we built is not a confidence in this sense.

\subsection{Symbolic reasoning and expert networks joined with neural networks}
Neuro symbolic computing~\cite{garcez2023neurosymbolic} couples a learned model with explicit rules,
and differentiable fuzzy logic makes the coupling trainable because the connectives become smooth
functions of continuous truth values~\cite{vankrieken2022analyzing}. In medical imaging the pattern
has generated spinal reports by pairing a generative network with a symbolic graph of domain
knowledge~\cite{han2021unifying} and predicted patient deterioration with a probabilistic logic
program over convolutional features~\cite{fadja2022neural}. Fuzzy membership
functions have been embedded in networks that process OCT directly, where a rough fuzzy
discretization guides the segmentation of fluid lesions~\cite{chen2023deep}, and pixel level fuzzy
rules have replaced learned feature extraction, so what the model attends to reads as a logical
statement~\cite{kim2025pixel}.

A parallel line builds an image model from cooperating experts. Steered mixtures of experts combine
constant experts through soft gating kernels with an explicit steering term~\cite{ozkan2023steered}, also for OCT speckle
removal~\cite{ozkan2024denoising}, and recent restoration networks route an input through experts of
different capacity according to its difficulty~\cite{zamfir2025complexity}. Outside imaging, evolving
fuzzy models identify actuator dynamics~\cite{precup2021evolving}, learned models are joined to a
physical forward operator in ultrasound tomography~\cite{fan2022model}, and sensitivity to initial
conditions is treated as a source of uncertainty in simulation~\cite{casquilho2025effect}. All rest
on the principle we rely on, that a learned component is easier to trust when part of the decision is
written in a language a person can read. What is missing is a rule layer that acts on the output of a strong denoiser,
names the clinical properties it protects, and limits how far it may move the image, which is the
object studied here.
