\section{Visual comparison for all four backbones}
\label{sec:supp_subjective}

Fig.~\ref{fig:supp_subjective} repeats the visual comparison of the main paper for every backbone
rather than the two extremes. The layout is the same. Every cell is a zoomed region chosen
automatically as the window with the strongest layer structure in the clean reference, because the
differences that matter sit at layer boundaries and are invisible at full scale. The last column
shows the change the wrapper applied, on a scale symmetric about zero, so the reader can see where
the correction acted rather than inferring it.

\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/fig_subjective_all.pdf}
\caption{Visual comparison on PKU37 for all four backbones. Columns are the noisy input, the backbone
output, the corrected output, the clean reference, and the change applied. Blue means the wrapper
lowered the value and orange means it raised it.}
\label{fig:supp_subjective}
\end{figure*}
